# Scientific and adversarial source audit

**Scope**. OSP 0.1.0a1 before review and 0.1.0a2 after incremental corrections. VERIFIED means observed or tested within the stated scope. PARTIALLY VERIFIED means restricted evidence is available. UNVERIFIED means the requisite evidence is absent. FAILED records a contradictory result, failed acceptance check, or blocked attempted operation with its reason.

| Scope and code | Finding and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Dark subtraction, `reduction.py`, `masters.py` | VERIFIED on controlled samples. An offset-bearing dark is subtracted once with no exposure scaling. Matching requires exposure, actual reported temperature, camera, mode, gain, offset, unit, ROI/binning, and configured settings. PARTIALLY VERIFIED physically because matched recorded bins do not guarantee the same silicon temperature, dark structure or thermal history. |
| Flat-dark lineage, `masters.py` | FAILED in a1. A previously built flat could bypass a stricter consumer temperature policy. a2 checks the flat-dark manifest type, exposure, detector signature, identity and full temperature range against the flat and the current policy. Regression test builds at a justified synthetic tolerance and rejects the same master with exact matching. |
| Flat field, `masters.py`, `combination.py` | VERIFIED on controlled homogeneous exposures. A translation-invariant estimator permits subtracting one shared flat-dark after combination; its variance is added once. Normalize by the finite unflagged median; reject low corrected response. FAILED in a1 because a constant clipped flat could enter an average. a2 rejects constant or wholly masked input flats. PARTIALLY VERIFIED for real sky/panel flats; changing flux and unknown nonlinear response can bias a mean master. No per-frame normalization or inferred detector threshold was introduced. |
| ERR, `reduction.py`, `products.py` | FAILED in a1 coverage labelling. With gain, the frame could claim conditional ERR with all or some NaNs. Finite zero could also come from a quantized dark stack. a2 emits unavailable, partial or conditional from actual usable coverage and rejects inconsistent labels on read. Zero or nonfinite temporal variance produces NaN ERR at that pixel. Conditional 1σ is a first-order marginal standard uncertainty under stated assumptions. |
| DQ, saturation and units, `dq.py`, `products.py` | VERIFIED for configured synthetic raw saturation/linearity, fatal-bit semantics and ADU FITS unit agreement. FAILED in a1 validator because a checksum-valid contradictory mask and finite SCI could pass. a2 rejects contradictions. UNVERIFIED physical threshold and linearity for Node 01. FITS product uses the common custom electron string for electron-valued data; Astropy's strict FITS unit parser rejects electron. Consumers should explicitly supply the electron unit through their CCDData reader. |
| QC and photometry, `quality.py` | VERIFIED within injected isolated Gaussian scene for PSF and centroids. PARTIALLY VERIFIED for actual field: source candidates are isolated peaks, Gaussian model flux is not calibrated photometry, severe blends are excluded and weights ignore covariance. A flat-field illumination transfer and extended emission can bias background estimates and apparent source completeness. |
| WCS and validation, `astrometry.py` | VERIFIED for synthetic coordinate origin, parity, SIP, wrong pointing, epoch motion and mocked retry. a2 converts tagged catalogue units instead of accepting raw values with incorrect physical interpretation and reports matched RMS before clipping and unmatched count. UNVERIFIED on-sky precision and false-association rate in dense fields; mutual-nearest matches and opaque user-supplied independence evidence cannot rule out chance associations. |
| Timing, `ingest.py` | VERIFIED observed DATE/MJD agreement within 1.007 ms. Averaged start/midpoint conventions differ from exposure-half predictions by roughly 0.58 s for lights. UNVERIFIED camera-clock latency, shutter/rolling readout timing and target coordinate precision. Original headers are retained. |
| Product linkage, `products.py`, `integrity.py` | FAILED in a1 semantic validation. a2 validates core FITS IDs against product provenance and rejects disagreement with published celestial WCS. SHA-256 and FITS checksums detect accidental corruption. A trusted master registry and filesystem are assumptions. Hashes do not establish authenticity against a party with write access. |
| Jobs, `jobs.py`, `job_lock.py` | PARTIALLY VERIFIED synthetic layers. Pixel buffers are per exposure. Job metadata grows with file count and each worker hashes both complete masters for every exposure. Concurrent edits to a registry or config after startup, power loss, detached subprocess cleanup and production-scale throughput remain risks. Performance findings are measured separately. |

## ERR semantics and assumptions

For an eligible pixel use N = raw − dark, flat response F and calibrated C = N/F. The ADU estimator is `(max(N,0)/g + V_temporal_dark + V_master_dark + C² V_flat)/F²`, with g in electrons per **stored** ADU. Electron inputs use g = 1 if values really represent electron counts from a linear calibration. Read and dark shot noise are included in independent matched dark temporal scatter. Adding a separate stand-alone read-noise term would count the same noise twice. The square root is written in the same unit as SCI.

- VERIFIED behavior. If an ADU input has no matching characterized gain, ERR is entirely NaN if a product can otherwise be calibrated. UNAVAILABLE conveys no numerical uncertainty. When some valid science pixels lack a usable variance, PARTIAL permits NaNs. Fatal DQ pixels have NaN SCI and ERR. A finite negative SCI can remain valid.
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED model. The per-pixel master and measurement terms are validated in synthetic independent cases. CONDITIONAL permits finite positive estimates but excludes shared normalization uncertainty, shared-master and spatial/readout covariance, gain error and systematic mismatch. The positive-count plug-in is biased upward close to zero. Scientifically interpretable coverage needs a measured valid gain and independent repeat observations.
- UNVERIFIED product for the 98 OHOBS exposures. The supplied configuration has no matching masters and no measured gain. There is no real ERR product. The camera-control setting GAIN=30 is not a conversion factor.
